Questions, answered.

Do I need any computer experience?

No. Our Microsoft Office Applications programme starts from the basics, and every programme is taught step by step. Tell us your level and we’ll recommend where to start.

Do I need my own laptop?

No. You’ll train on the workstations in our lab. If you already have a laptop, ask us about using it in class.

How much do the courses cost?

Fees depend on the programme and schedule you choose. Call or WhatsApp 0802 959 6214 for current fees.

How long does each course take?

It depends on the programme and how often you attend. We’ll give you a clear timeline when you enrol.

Can I use the cybercafé without enrolling?

Yes. Internet access, printing and photocopying are open to everyone — just walk in.

Where exactly are you?

Ogunfayo Road, Eputu Town, Ibeju-Lekki, Lagos 105101 — near Eputu Mall, just off the Lekki–Epe Expressway. Get directions on Google Maps.
