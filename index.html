FAQ

Questions we get asked at every intake.

Can't find your answer here? Reach us directly and we'll respond same day.

Ask us directly
Do I need prior experience to start?
No. Every track starts from fundamentals. If you already have experience, we assess and place you into an intermediate cohort instead.
Are classes in-person or online?
Both. Choose in-person at our Lagos training rooms, live online, or a hybrid schedule — same curriculum either way.
Do you offer installment payment plans?
Yes, every track can be split into 2–3 payments. Corporate cohorts are invoiced on agreed milestones.
Will I get a certificate?
Yes — issued on completion of your final project, alongside a portfolio review and reference letter on request.
Can I use the office space without enrolling in a course?
Yes, coworking desks and meeting rooms are open to the public on daily, weekly or monthly plans. See the Office Space page for pricing.
