Courses

Four courses, all taught in person at our lab.

  1. Web Design & Development Plan, design, code and publish websites. You finish with a live site of your own. HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Git
  2. Graphics Design Logos, flyers, banners and brand materials, prepared properly for print and screen. CorelDRAW, Photoshop
  3. Microsoft Office Applications From computer basics to confident work in the programs every office uses. Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Access
  4. Database Management Design databases that keep records organised, accurate and easy to search. Microsoft Access, SQL

Web Design & Development

Your first website, written line by line.

On the web design course you write the code yourself, in a proper code editor, from the first class. Then you learn to make it work on phones, add interactivity with JavaScript and put it online for anyone to visit.

Questions

Before you enrol.

Do I need any computer experience?

No. The Microsoft Office course starts from the basics, and every course is taught step by step. Tell us where you’re starting from and we’ll suggest the right course.

Do I need my own laptop?

No. You’ll practise on the desktop computers in our lab. If you have a laptop, ask us about bringing it to class.

How much are the fees?

Fees depend on the course and the schedule you choose. Call or WhatsApp 0802 959 6214 for current fees.

How long does a course take?

It depends on the course and how often you attend. We’ll give you a clear timeline when you enrol.

Can I use the cybercafé without enrolling?

Yes. Internet access, printing and photocopying are open to everyone.

Where exactly are you?

Ogunfayo Road, Eputu Town, Ibeju-Lekki, near Eputu Mall and just off the Lekki-Epe Expressway. Get directions on Google Maps.
