Web Design & Development

Learn how websites are planned, designed, built and published. You write real HTML, CSS and JavaScript from the first class, and finish with a live website of your own.

What you’ll learn

  1. How the web worksBrowsers, servers, domain names and hosting, explained simply.
  2. HTML foundationsHeadings, links, images, lists and forms.
  3. Styling with CSSColour, type, spacing, Flexbox and Grid.
  4. Responsive designPages that look right on phones, tablets and laptops.
  5. JavaScript essentialsVariables, events and the DOM, to make pages interactive.
  6. From design to codeTurning a layout into a clean, accessible page.
  7. Tools of the tradeVS Code, browser developer tools, Git and GitHub.
  8. Going livePublishing your site so anyone can visit it.

By the end, you can

  • Build a multi-page website from a blank file
  • Make any layout work on phones and laptops
  • Add interactivity with JavaScript
  • Publish and update a live website
  • Show a portfolio of work you built yourself

Tools

HTML, CSS, JavaScript, VS Code, Git and GitHub

Suited to

Complete beginners, students and school leavers, business owners who want their own website, and future freelancers.

Graphics Design

Design the things every business needs: logos, flyers, banners and social media posts. Learn the principles that make design look professional, and the software to produce it.

What you’ll learn

  1. Design principlesLayout, balance, contrast and hierarchy.
  2. Colour and typeChoosing colours and fonts that work together.
  3. CorelDRAW essentialsShapes, text, vectors and page layout.
  4. Logos and brand identityLogos, business cards and letterheads.
  5. Photoshop essentialsPhoto editing, retouching and compositing.
  6. Flyers, banners and postersDesigns for print and for social media.
  7. Preparing for printSizes, resolution, bleed and exporting for the printer.
  8. Portfolio projectA complete brand kit for a real or imagined business.

By the end, you can

  • Design a logo and matching brand materials
  • Edit and retouch photos cleanly
  • Prepare artwork correctly for print
  • Create graphics for social media
  • Present your work in a portfolio

Tools

CorelDRAW and Adobe Photoshop

Suited to

Creative beginners, business centre operators, small business owners, and anyone who manages social media for a brand.

Course 4 of 4

UI/UX Design

Learn how great digital products are designed. You research real users, sketch ideas, build wireframes and turn them into polished, clickable prototypes in Figma.

What you’ll learn

  1. What UI and UX meanHow good design makes apps and websites easy to use.
  2. User researchInterviews, personas and understanding real needs.
  3. User flows and wireframesPlanning screens and journeys before visual design.
  4. Figma essentialsFrames, components, auto layout and styles.
  5. Visual designColour, type, spacing and layout for screens.
  6. PrototypingClickable prototypes that feel like the real thing.
  7. Usability testingTesting with users and improving what you learn.
  8. Portfolio case studyA complete project you can show employers and clients.

By the end, you can

  • Research users and define the problem
  • Plan flows and wireframes
  • Design clean, consistent screens in Figma
  • Build and test clickable prototypes
  • Present your work as a portfolio case study

Tools

Figma, FigJam and simple paper sketching

Suited to

Creative beginners, graphic designers moving into digital, web design students, and anyone curious about building apps.
